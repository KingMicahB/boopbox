BoopBox Privacy Policy

Effective 4 November 2026

In short: BoopBox has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no servers of its own. Your cards, songs and settings stay on your phone. BoopBox only talks to other services when it needs to for a feature you use, like looking up artwork or playing a link.

This policy covers the BoopBox Android app (com.boopboxapp), made by Micah King ("I", "me"). It explains what the app does with information and what it doesn't do.

What I collect

Nothing. BoopBox doesn't send me any personal information, usage statistics, crash reports or device identifiers, and it has no advertising or tracking code.

What stays on your phone

  • Your cards: names, pictures, songs, links, podcast feeds, and where each one stopped playing.
  • Audio and artwork you add or download, and cached podcast episodes.
  • Your settings.
  • If you connect a Yoto account, the sign-in tokens Yoto gives the app.

Uninstalling BoopBox deletes all of this. If Android backup is on for your phone, Android may include BoopBox's data in your own Google backup. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account, not by me.

Services BoopBox connects to

Some features need the internet. When you use them, BoopBox connects straight from your phone to the service involved. Each service gets the request it needs (your IP address and what you asked for) under its own privacy policy:

  • Apple iTunes Search: the search words you type when you look for cover art.
  • YouTube, Spotify and Netflix: a link you added, so BoopBox can show its title and picture, or play a YouTube video inside the app.
  • Podcast hosts: the feeds and episodes on your podcast cards.
  • Image and audio links you add: whichever site the link points to.
  • Yoto (only if you choose to connect it): you sign in on Yoto's own page in your browser. BoopBox gets read-only access to your family's Yoto library so it can play those cards. You can disconnect in BoopBox settings at any time.
  • Google Play: purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. I never see your payment details.

When a card opens another app, such as Spotify, YouTube or Audible, that app's own privacy policy applies.

Permissions

  • NFC: to read and write the cards you tap.
  • Internet: for the services listed above.
  • Notifications and background playback: so audio keeps playing with the screen off and you get playback controls.
  • Display over other apps: so BoopBox can return to the front after it opens another app.
  • Notification access (optional, only if you turn it on): lets BoopBox pause or resume what another app is playing. BoopBox doesn't read, store or share your notifications.

Sharing cards

When you export or share a card, BoopBox makes a .boop file holding that card's contents and gives it to the app you pick. Where it goes after that is up to you.

Children

BoopBox is made for families, and children can use it. It doesn't collect personal information from anyone, children included, and it has no chat, accounts or ads. Signing in to Yoto and making purchases happen on Yoto's and Google's own screens, which a parent controls.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
